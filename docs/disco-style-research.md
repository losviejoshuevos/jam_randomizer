# Disco style research

## Scope

The sample spans orchestral 1970s disco, disco-funk, Euro/Italo direction, post-disco boogie and modern nu-disco. The generator focuses on live-band harmony suitable for a jam rather than production-specific orchestration.

## Resulting internal archetypes

1. `classic-70s`: four-on-the-floor compatible functional loops and orchestral/soul color.
2. `disco-funk`: one/two-chord riff-led themes and compact releases.
3. `italo-disco`: minor/pop four-chord cycles at a slightly faster pulse.
4. `boogie-post-disco`: bass-led, slightly slower vamps with seventh/ninth color.
5. `nu-disco`: modern pop cycles, loopability and restrained borrowed color.

## Generator constraints

- Meter is 4/4 for the style profile.
- BPM concentrates around 104–128 depending on archetype.
- More chords do not imply more energy: high BPM patterns remain simpler.
- Diminished/altered vocabulary is rare; 6, m7, maj7, 9, 13 and sus dominate.
- Section B/C should change lift or tonal emphasis while keeping a stable dance loop.
